Frequently Asked Questions

What types of staffing does Clevanoo offer?

We provide contract, contract-to-hire, and direct placement for technology roles. See our staffing services for details.

How do I apply for a role?

Browse current openings, open the job description, and select Apply. The form accepts a PDF, DOC, or DOCX résumé up to 2 MB.

Can you help with an existing Zendesk setup?

Yes. Our Zendesk services include platform assessments, integrations, automation, reporting, and ongoing optimization.

How do I stop receiving emails?

Use the unsubscribe form to request removal.
